A WAY INTO ART

Start with a feeling.
Follow your curiosity.

  1. Feel

    Choose an atmosphere. Each feeling opens a suggested route through catalogue themes.

    Answer a few optional questions. See the artworks change, skip a question, or undo any choice.

    Open research filters for an artist, a date range, or a type of work. Switch to the list to compare catalogue details.

Prototype coverage: feeling routes are editorial theme suggestions, not measured emotions. Visual comparison uses the small indexed sample. Artwork DNA, when available, is a model-assisted reading. Titles, dates and media come from NGA.
